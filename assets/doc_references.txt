PHISHING AWARENESS PROGRAM - ACADEMIC STUDY NOTES
=================================================

This project is built as an educational portal to raise cybersecurity awareness.
Here are reference sources and details on Phishing taxonomy:

1. Phishing Overview:
   Phishing represents a form of social engineering where attackers manipulate users into acting against their own security interests.
   
2. Core Categories of Social Engineering:
   - Authority: Impersonating IT admins, bank authorities, or government agents.
   - Urgency: Threatening account suspension, parcel returns, or penalties within tight timeframes (e.g., 24 hours).
   - Social Proof: Tricking targets into believing others have already performed the action.
   - Scarcity: Offering limited-time benefits or items to spark quick clicks.

3. Reference Guidelines:
   - NIST SP 800-53 (Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems)
   - OWASP Top 10 Client-Side Phishing & Impersonation Mitigation standards.
   - SANS Security Awareness Training recommendations.
