What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical Hacking (also known as Penetration Testing or White Hat Hacking) involves authorized attempts to gain unauthorized access to a computer system, application, or data. Unlike malicious hackers, ethical hackers operate under strict legal authorization and scope to discover vulnerabilities before threat actors can exploit them.

The Hacker Matrix: White, Black & Grey Hats

White Hat

Ethical & Legal

Authorized security professionals employed by companies to find and report vulnerabilities responsibly. Operate strictly within scope contracts.

Black Hat

Malicious & Illegal

Cybercriminals who breach systems without permission to steal sensitive data, deploy ransomware, or profit financially.

Grey Hat

Uncertain Authorization

Security researchers who probe systems without prior permission, but without malicious intent. They report bugs, sometimes demanding rewards.
